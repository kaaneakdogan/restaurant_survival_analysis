I have seen many people, usually middle-income families, trying to open their dream restaurant. 
But oftentimes, people fail for various reasons. Whether this be lack of experience, luck, or financial constraints,
the aim of this project is to provide to those who want to accomplish their dreams an advantage that is
set in stone. One advantage provided on the condition of a decision made only once, where the fruits of it can be reaped
for generations to come. 

My family opened a bakery a decade ago, and it was very successful. After they opened that bakery, many businesses flourished in the area,
and that neighborhood, which was once made up of a tiny grocery store and some offices, now includes a gym, dozens of restaurants, 2 other
grocery stores, barbers, a mosque, a school and a daycare center. 

I wish the findings of the project can help you succeed in your dream. The next step of this project is to predict development of a
neighborhood based on reviews. Maybe it is less the people present, but more so what comes after, signaled by the reviews of people. 
This is something I will explore in the future. 

That is why this project matters.
